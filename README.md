# HerRise AI – AI-Powered Women's Financial Empowerment Platform

> **Tagline:** Her Future. Her Finance. Her Freedom.

HerRise AI is a modern, unified web platform designed to empower women across every phase of their economic journey. It combines personal financial management, career acceleration and returnships, micro-enterprise incubation, transparent funding discovery, a women-led marketplace, certified skill academies, and verified 1:1 mentorship into one cohesive, accessible ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Features

### 1. Landing Page (`/`)
- **Brand Hero:** Headline *"Your Dreams Deserve a Platform. Your Future Deserves HerRise."* with supportive narrative and visual mockups.
- **Platform Demo Statistics:** 120,000+ Women Empowered, 45,000+ Career Opportunities, 18,500+ Businesses Supported, and 850+ Learning Resources.
- **7-Module Showcase:** Interactive cards with deep links to all platform disciplines.
- **How HerRise AI Works:** 4-step progressive journey (Profile, Goals, Opportunities, Growth).
- **Why Choose HerRise AI:** Pillars of trust, privacy, returnships, and regional language inclusion.
- **Testimonials & Success Stories:** Real-feeling transformation stories and member reviews.
- **FAQ Accordion & Footer:** Common questions, demo disclaimer, language switcher, and platform links.

### 2. Authentication & Onboarding
- **Login (`/login`):** Email & password, remember me, forgot password modal, and Google SSO visual option.
- **Registration (`/register`):** Full name, email, mobile number, location, password, and preferred language.
- **Onboarding Wizard (`/onboarding`):** 3-step personalization collecting goals (Finances, Job, Career Restart, Startup, Funding, Marketplace, Skills, Mentorship), educational background, experience, skills tags, and regional language with confetti celebration.

### 3. Main Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- **Personalized Header:** Greeting with user's selected goals and profile completion strength bar.
- **4 Core Metrics:** Monthly inflow, liquid savings cushion, career matches, and HerMarket sales.
- **Quick Actions:** One-click shortcuts for all 7 modules.
- **Interactive Analytics:** 6-month Income vs Expense bar chart (Recharts).
- **In-Progress Learning Card:** Active course progress tracking with resume lesson button.
- **Upcoming Masterclass:** Registered workshop card with mentor details and countdown.
- **Recent Activities:** Store orders and financial transactions.

### 4. HerFinance – Financial Management (`/finance`)
- **Financial Dashboard:** Total Inflow, Total Outflow, Current Savings, and Net Cashflow Surplus.
- **Emergency Fund Health Indicator:** Automatic 6-month living cost calculation against current liquid reserves.
- **Interactive Budgeting & Expense Matrix:** Category-wise expense allocation pie chart and 6-month trend.
- **Savings Goals:** Visual progress bars with "+ Deposit" simulation modal.
- **Add Income & Add Expense Modals:** Dynamic ledger updates with instant chart recalculation.
- **Financial Literacy Lessons:** Micro-learning reader on 50/30/20 budgeting, emergency funds, and SIPs.
- **Conversational Finance AI Coach:** Embedded widget answering taxation and budgeting questions.
- **Monthly Financial Health Report:** Printable summary modal with download capability.

### 5. HerCareer – Employment & Returnships (`/career`)
- **Job Discovery Engine:** Search and multi-criteria filters for Remote/WFH, Full-time, Part-time, and Returnships.
- **Returnee-Friendly Highlight:** Specialized opportunities for women returning from career sabbaticals.
- **Interactive Resume Builder:** 
  - Sections for Contact, Career Break Framing, Summary, Experience, Skills, and Certifications.
  - Template Switcher: Modern, Minimal, Executive.
  - Live formatted preview pane with Download / Print functionality.
- **Career Restart Programme:** Dedicated track addressing sabbaticals with confidence.
- **Skill-Gap Analysis:** Benchmark user skills against target roles (Growth Marketing, Financial Analyst, Tech).
- **STAR-Method Interview Prep:** Realistic answers for explaining career breaks and behavioral scenarios.

### 6. HerBusiness – Entrepreneurship Studio (`/business`)
- **Curated Business Ideas:** Low-barrier, high-margin business models with initial capital and profit margins.
- **Interactive Feasibility Quiz:** Diagnostic assessment calculating launch viability score out of 100.
- **Pricing & Profit Margin Calculator:** Cost-plus and value-based pricing tool with sliders for raw materials, packaging, labor, shipping, and margin targets, calculating recommended retail price and monthly breakeven units.
- **10-Section AI Business Plan Generator:** Fillable investor-grade business plan with export/print capability.
- **30-Day Launch Roadmap:** Interactive week-by-week milestone checklist.
- **AI Business Strategy Coach:** Quick assistant answering go-to-market queries.

### 7. HerFund – Transparent Funding Discovery (`/fund`)
- **Funding Scheme Cards:** Verified demo schemes across Public Banks, NBFCs, Women Grants, Crowdfunding, and Angel Equity.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix:** Compare interest rates, collateral requirements, processing speeds, and loan limits.
- **Pre-Flight Document Checklist:** Track readiness of PAN, Aadhaar, ITR, and Bank Statements with upload simulation.
- **6-Slide Investor Pitch Deck Builder:** Slide-by-slide creator (Problem, Solution, TAM, Model, Projections, Ask) with export.
- **Demo Application Modal:** Direct capital request simulation.

### 8. HerMarket – Products & Services Marketplace (`/market`)
- **Marketplace Storefront:** Products across Fashion, Handmade, Beauty, Home Decor, Food, Services, and Digital.
- **Product Details Modal:** Image preview, seller information, customer ratings, and specs.
- **Shopping Cart & Checkout:** Cart drawer with quantity adjusters, subtotal calculation, and simulated demo checkout.
- **Seller Dashboard:** Gross sales stats, fulfilled orders, revenue chart, order status updates (Processing, Shipped, Delivered), and Add Product modal.

### 9. HerLearn – Skill Academy & Certifications (`/learn`)
- **Course Catalog:** 8 specialized categories with ratings, instructor bios, duration, and enrollment.
- **Video Lesson Player:** Simulated HD player with module playlist, progress tracking, and "Mark Complete" button.
- **Interactive Knowledge Quiz:** Real-time scoring, explanations, and confetti celebration upon passing.
- **Certificates Hub:** Issued verified credentials with certificate preview and PDF download simulation.
- **30-Day Learning Sprint:** Structured weekly milestones based on user goals.

### 10. HerNetwork – Mentorship & Community (`/network`)
- **Mentor Discovery:** Filter female leaders by Industry (Investment, D2C, Tech, IP Law) and language.
- **1:1 Mentorship Booking Flow:** Select date, select 30-min slot, enter topic, and receive instant booking ticket.
- **Live Masterclasses:** Register for upcoming live weekend workshops with state toggle.
- **Community Discussion Feed:** Post updates, share founder wins, give likes, and reply to sisterhood comments.
- **Success Stories Showcase:** Transformation case studies.

### 11. Unified AI Assistant (`/ai-assistant`)
- Dedicated full-page conversational interface.
- Category filters for Finance, Career, Business, Funding, Market, and Learning.
- Predefined prompt library and realistic AI answers with calculations and formatted markdown.
- Simulated voice input toggle, clear chat, and clipboard copy.

### 12. Profile & Settings (`/profile` & `/settings`)
- **Profile:** Manage name, email, phone, location, status, business name, bio, and add/remove skills pills.
- **Settings:** Language selector (English, हिन्दी, मराठी), notification alert toggles, privacy preferences, and demo reset.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **React 19** with **Vite**
- **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS v4** (`@tailwindcss/vite`)
- **React Router DOM v7**
- **Lucide React** (Modern accessible icon set)
- **Recharts** (Bar charts, pie charts, tooltips)
- **Canvas-Confetti** (Milestone and quiz celebrations)
- **LocalStorage Persistence** (All demo state, cart, transactions, and settings persist across page reloads)

## 🚀 Running the Project Locally

```bash
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Run the Vite development server
npm run dev

# 3. Or build for production and preview
npm run build
npm run preview
```

Open `http://localhost:5173/` in your browser.
